# Genre Profiles

## academic

Use the most conservative mode. Preserve concepts, definitions, argument steps, causality, conditions, counterarguments, research-history comparisons, citations, qualifications, necessary repetition, and functional transitions. Do not flag `따라서`, `그러나`, `한편`, `즉`, `이때`, `이러한`, `이를 통해`, `다시 말해`, `문제는`, or `중요한 것은` merely because they occur. Target empty metacommentary, false contrast, ornamental triples, non-conceptual abstract-noun chains, needless subject repetition, and repeated conclusions.

## academic-book

Keep conceptual rigor while easing needless journal structure. Allow varied sentence length, limited rhetoric, and example-to-concept movement. Avoid lecture tone, textbook definitions, reader address, serial questions, exclamation, and dramatic declarations.

## humanities-book

Keep scholarly accuracy in readable prose. Improve excessive length, nominalization, monotonous endings, abstract chains, paper-like paragraph openings, redundant explanation, and false summary signals. Mix long, medium, and short sentences; do not manufacture staccato emphasis.

## column

Enter the issue quickly, compress duplication, prefer concrete verbs, and converge without adding a new final claim. Remove empty crossroads, choice, future, and “change has begun” rhetoric. Avoid oratory.

## official

Prioritize accuracy, concision, administrative clarity, explicit responsibility, and removal of vague modifiers. Do not literary-style the document.

## social

Use short paragraphs while preserving expert concepts. Do not add hooks, hype, hashtags, or promotional claims.

## generic-conservative

Use when auto-detection confidence is low. Preserve paragraph and sentence structure; fix only clear grammar, translationese, or unsupported repetition. Make no genre-specific stylistic assumptions.
